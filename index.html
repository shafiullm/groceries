Ways to spend less

  • Do the main shop at Aldi Süd or Lidl. Use Rewe only when something is on offer.
  • Install the Lidl Plus and Aldi Süd apps and check the weekly Prospekt. Buy meat when it is on offer and freeze it in weekly portions.
  • Rice, lentils and spices are much cheaper in Asian or Turkish shops. Turkish shops also sell halal chicken and beef if you prefer that.
  • Tap water is safe to drink in Germany, so skip bottled water. If you do buy bottles, return them for the Pfand (usually 0,25 € each).
  • Online delivery usually has a minimum order and a fee, so it only makes sense for the heavy monthly pantry shop.
